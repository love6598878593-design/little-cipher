Before you join

A growing library,
without more screen time.

Is this an app or a printable product?

Both. Members can play from the private library on any device or download beautifully typeset US Letter and A4 editions for screen-free play.

What happens after the free trial?

Monthly Club renews at $12 per month and Annual Family at $89 per year after 14 days. We email you before the first charge, and you can cancel from your parent account at any time. Starter Pack and School License do not include a trial.

Does the library adapt to each child?

Yes. Recommendations use age, interests, completed sets, and parent feedback. Family Salon keeps a separate path for each child.
